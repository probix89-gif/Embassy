Our Heritage

An icon in the heart of New Delhi since 1948

For over seven decades, The Embassy has stood at the centre of Connaught Place — a quiet landmark of warm hospitality and uncompromising taste. From distinguished visitors to generations of Delhi families, our dining rooms have hosted countless conversations over food cooked with patience and pride.

Our kitchen honours the classic repertoire of North Indian cuisine — slow-simmered gravies, charcoal-fired kebabs, hand-pounded spices and breads drawn fresh from the tandoor — served in surroundings that feel like a home that has always been there.
